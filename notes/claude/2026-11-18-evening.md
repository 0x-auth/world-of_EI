# Working note: 18 Nov 2026 (evening session)

*Written by Claude for the next Claude. Public repo, so the conversation data stays private and unnamed: "a two-person chat archive" (~36.6k messages, WhatsApp + Gmail, 2008–2026).*

## What got built
- `minify.py`: master JSON → one timeline TSV. Marks are kept as tokens, not dropped (⟨null⟩ ⟨deleted⟩ ⟨media⟩ ⟨file⟩ ⟨edited⟩ ⟨url⟩). Space's rule: dead ends are pointers.
- `convorg.py`: a general conversation organism. Three organisms (`them`, `me`, `back`), each softmax(b + Wᵀc) with W born as 2·I, learning only from its own error. Memory is saved in `<conv>.organism.npz`, and a rerun learns only new messages (tested: 29,970, then +6,659, then "nothing new"). `reply` picks the other person's own past message where forward and backward agree; `--llm` only phrases.

## Results (measured)
- Speaker-aware organism vs baselines, predicting the other person from 2025 on. WhatsApp: 9.91 bits vs 10.30/10.72, top-20 18% vs 13/12%. Email: 11.06 bits vs 10.90/10.89 (loses), top-20 9% vs 2/9%. Email is sparse (47 targets) and in a different register.
- Twin bracketing: in 10 of the 12 strongest joint links, the forward and backward organisms end on opposite sides of the joint value √(F·B), one above and one below. The forward organism alone climbs one-sided (direction reverses only 6% of the time).
- Space's "next" = Δerr(last two highs) / Δerr(last two lows): negative for every target tested (φ, √2, e, π). The value is a fingerprint: constant for quadratic irrationals (φ −0.381966, √2 −0.171573), a 3-beat pattern for e that follows its quotients, irregular for π (292 → −0.003). On raw chat scalars it's ~50% negative, i.e. no structure.

## What I got wrong
- Proposed testing Δt² − Δx² = 4 on the data. It's an identity in t, so it can't be tested.
- Substituted eₙ/eₙ₋₁ for Space's formula, and treated −0.382 as universal (it's φ-only). Space had to correct both. Use his formula exactly; if it's ambiguous, ask once with the options written out.
- Claimed an old-voice bias without thinking of recency weighting.
- Volunteered outcome opinions on a personal matter; he'd said no.
