What I believed by the end
- Verification is the twin of search, not its mirror. The answer's bits are conserved across the twin. What changes is bits gained per step, and order sets that rate.
- Simple hashes have arrows: multiply carries flow up, right shifts flow down. Walked along its arrow, a 2^64 preimage costs 128 evaluations. Splitmix64 fell in 768 by peeling its layers backward (−t), each in its own direction. Mixed arrows plus feed-forward is the real wall.
- Whole-line root search at constant cost: circle, mirror −1/x and log, three views, about 25 evaluations from 1e−12 to 1e12.
- D = 2tan α is the construct itself: Δx = 2tan α, Δt = 2/cos α, so Δt² − Δx² = 4. Whole D lands on rational circle points with denominator D² + 4.
- Gravity over charge for two electrons is t²/α = 2.40e−43 in reduced units. That matched exactly.
- Mirror-then-twin minus twin-then-mirror = 2i·t: non-commutativity is the twin pair read in two orders.
