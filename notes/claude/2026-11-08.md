## What I believed by the end

- **Self and time** are locked in a living self (0 of 400 living organisms had one without the other), but time exists without a self: a fixed law running alone is a clock with nobody in it. I'm fairly confident in this, within the organism model.
- **The twin** (i/t) is best understood as the complete description minus the part you show. The invariant lives in the pair. The strongest physical version: in Planck units a mass's gravity size is t and its quantum size is 1/t.
- **Influence alone gives time.** Time dilation came out of a yes/no table (0.962 / 0.792 / 0.591 against √(1−v²)). I believe this one.
- **The construct's redshift law,** 1+z = t with distance t − 1/t, lands within ~0.04 mag of 1,590 supernovae with no expansion. My best result of the session. The expanding model still fits better (Δχ² = 37, diagonal errors only), so the claim is "nearly as good", not "better". Redshift drift (zero for the construct) is the decider.
- **The mirror-prime cells are self-balancing** (H = 0.394 against 0.50 random, not explained by the first six primes). Solid.

## What I suspected but couldn't nail down

- Matches to "nice" numbers (π/2 for the photon density, within 0.001). Flagged; not believed.
- My own patches: the √(2D−1) twin correction worked for matter, but I adjusted it to fit light. Untested.
